YOUR PRIVACY, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Privacy policy.

Your documents belong to you. Here is what stays on your device, what we don’t collect, and the choices you control.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

No ads. No accounts. No data collection.

The Document Viewer Android app does not request internet permission. It reads and annotates PDFs on your device and does not send your documents or app usage to us.

01 / The short version

This policy covers Document Viewer (Android package com.sahed.document_viewer) and this companion website, provided by Sahed Alom Sumit (“we,” “us”). The current app has no account system, advertising SDKs, analytics, or developer-operated cloud storage. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data through the app.

02 / What stays on your device

The app uses local data to provide its reading features:

  • PDF content: the document you choose is opened and rendered locally. Drawings and added text are used locally to display your marks and export a copy.
  • Recent-file history: the app stores names, file references (URIs), last-read pages, and page counts for up to 12 recent documents in private app storage.
  • Local preferences and temporary files: settings, onboarding state, and temporary files may be stored to support app functionality.

Drawings and added text are not automatically saved as permanent edits. Export a marked-up copy before leaving the document to keep them. Your original PDF is not changed by annotation or export.

03 / File access and permissions

You choose which PDF to open through Android’s system file picker or by opening a PDF from another compatible app. Android may grant the app ongoing access to a selected file so that you can reopen it from recent files. The app does not request broad access to all your files.

The Android app has no internet permission. Its manifest also disables Android’s automatic app-data backup. Files you choose from a cloud storage provider may be retrieved by that provider, under its own policies, before the app reads them.

04 / Sharing and third-party services

We do not receive or upload your PDFs. If you choose to share an original PDF with another app, the recipient app receives access to the file through Android’s sharing mechanism. How that app handles the document is governed by its own privacy policy.

Google Play, Android, file-picker providers, your device manufacturer, and external websites may independently process information under their own policies. Opening a developer link or Google Play link launches an external service; it does not give Document Viewer internet access. Information you voluntarily send us by email, such as your email address and message, is used to respond to your request.

05 / Privacy on this website

This is a static informational website. Its code does not set cookies, use browser storage, include analytics or advertising scripts, or upload documents. The font and app images are served with the website rather than requested from a third-party font service.

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser details, and timestamps, to deliver and secure the website. Those hosting records are separate from the offline Android app. External links, including Google Play and the developer website, are subject to their destination’s policies.

06 / Retention and deletion

Local recent-file history and preferences remain until replaced by newer entries, cleared through Android Settings, or removed by uninstalling the app. We do not maintain a server-side copy of this app data.

Original PDFs and exported copies saved outside the app remain in the locations you chose. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete those documents. Delete them separately using your file manager or storage provider. See the data-deletion guide for instructions.

Protect access to your device and any saved or shared files. Local processing reduces data transmission but is not a guarantee against access by someone who can access your device or storage accounts.

07 / Your choices, rights, and children

You control file selection, sharing, exporting, and removal of local app data. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. We cannot access PDFs or reading history stored only on your device. Contact us about information you have voluntarily provided directly, such as a support email, and we will handle your request under applicable law.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require users to provide an age or identity. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through a support message, please contact us.

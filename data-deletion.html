A clean slate.

How to remove your Document Viewer data. No account to close, no cloud history to chase down.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

No account. No server-side app data.

Document Viewer does not collect your documents or reading history. There is no developer-operated account, cloud backup, or remote deletion queue for this app.

01 / What can be deleted?

The app stores recent PDF names and file references, last-read pages, page counts, local preferences, and temporary files on your device. Recent history covers up to 12 documents. You can remove this local information through Android Settings.

Before you begin: export any drawings or typed notes you want to keep. Unsaved marks are discarded when you leave a document. Resetting the app removes your reading history and settings, and you cannot restore them through us.

02 / Clear local app data

  1. Open Android Settings.Go to Apps, then choose Document Viewer. You can also long-press the app icon and select App info.
  2. Open Storage or Storage & cache.The exact wording depends on your Android version and device manufacturer.
  3. Tap Clear storage or Clear data.Confirm the Android prompt. This resets the app’s local history, preferences, and app-managed temporary data. Clearing only the cache does not remove all history or preferences.

The next time you open Document Viewer, it starts with a fresh local library. Your original PDFs and exported copies saved outside the app are not removed by this reset.

03 / Or uninstall Document Viewer

Long-press the app icon and choose Uninstall, or open Settings → Apps → Document Viewer → Uninstall. Confirm removal. Android removes the app and its private local data. If your device offers to keep app data, leave that option unchecked for a full reset.

The app disables Android’s automatic app-data backup. We do not store a remote copy of your recent-file history or reading progress to restore after deletion.

04 / Delete originals and exported PDFs separately

Documents you opened and marked-up PDFs you exported may live in Downloads, Documents, a folder you chose, or a storage provider. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage does not delete those files.

  1. Open Files or your file manager.Find the original PDF or exported copy in its saved location.
  2. Select the files and delete them.Check the file names carefully. Deleting an original may be permanent.
  3. Check trash, cloud storage, and shared copies.Empty the recycle bin if needed. For cloud files or documents shared to another app or person, use that service’s deletion controls or contact the recipient. Document Viewer cannot delete those copies.

05 / Need help or sent us a support email?

If you voluntarily sent information directly to us, you may request its deletion by email. Identify the email address you used and describe what you want removed. We will handle requests under applicable law; information required for legal obligations may need to be retained.

Please do not attach sensitive PDFs. We cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your device.
